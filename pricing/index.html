Pricing

Priced on what your inbox actually does, not on how many people look at it.

No table, because a desk taking forty orders a day and one taking four hundred are not the same work. We look at your inbox, then quote.

What moves the number

  • Volume

    How much lands, and in what form. The first thing we measure.

  • Inboxes

    How many the desk runs, shared and personal.

  • Systems

    Reading your ERP is included. Writing into it is scoped.

  • Paperwork

    A standard security review is included. A custom one is scoped.

What you never pay for

  • Users. Invite the whole company, at no extra cost.
  • All 25+ languages, and every system Hoshii reads from.
  • Corrections. Being wrong once is how it learns, not an extra.

How it starts

Read only. Hoshii prepares and proposes, and nothing leaves until someone approves it. Your own analytics then show which processes are worth automating, and the quote moves with the ones you pick. You never buy an automation you have not watched work.

The work itself is metered in credits, on a monthly allowance we size to an average month with you. Unused credits roll over.

Bring one real inbox. We will work out the number with you.
