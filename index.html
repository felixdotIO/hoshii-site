Hoshii is the AI‑native inbox purpose‑built to enable B2B operations teams to communicate faster and with fewer errors. We proactively draft client‑specific replies, suggest areas of improvement and take care of the repetitive but necessary work in your day.

Your team stops sorting and re-typing. It starts deciding.

  1. 01 Connect

    One inbox for the whole team, live this afternoon.

    Shared and personal mailboxes connect through Outlook SSO. No migration, no new address, and replies still go out from yours. Over 1,300 connectors bring the surrounding systems in with them.

    • Nothing to migrate

      Sign in with Outlook, pick the mailboxes, done. 1,300+ connectors bring ERP and CRM context in with them.

    • Nobody forwards mail again

      Reassign a thread, @mention a colleague, answer in place. Everyone can see who has what.

    • Private until you share it

      Personal mailboxes stay yours until you share one, and every change is logged. GDPR‑compliant, and an easy conversation with your works council.

  2. 02 Orchestrate

    Sorted, routed, looked up. Before you open it.

    An orchestrating worker reads every arriving email, decides where it belongs and what it needs, then fetches exactly that from your connected tools and uploaded knowledge, with read access only.

    • Every email lands sorted

      Classified, prioritised and put in front of the right person.

    • It decides what to look up

      Customer, order, stock, credit. Pulled from your connected tools and uploaded knowledge.

    • Every fact shows its source

      Stamped with the system it came from and the time it was read.

  3. 03 Learn

    Correct it once. It works that way from then on.

    It learns from every correction, not only the ones you make to text. Reroute a task, add the lookup it missed, edit a draft before you send: each of those changes how the next email like it is handled.

    • Replies are drafted, never sent

      Every reply waits for a human. Nothing sends itself.

    • Corrections are the training

      A reroute, a missed lookup, an edited draft. Each one changes what happens next.

    • It learns per customer

      14 corrections for Meier AG: how their orders are read, looked up and answered.

  4. 04 Anticipate

    See where work piles up.

    Open quotes with no reply come back as a proposed follow‑up. Missing order details get chased, delivery dates get nudged. Proposed first, always.

    • Quotes that went quiet

      Four days without a reply, and the follow‑up is already drafted and waiting.

    • Chases you never started

      Missing delivery dates get asked for. Late shipments get a nudge.

    • Ask before you approve

      If you are unsure about a suggestion, ask it right there. It answers from the same context it used to make it.

  5. 05 Measure

    Email stops being a black box.

    Response times, volumes by type, handling times, what came in late and where it stalled. On top of it an opportunity map built from your own live data: which task types are worth automating, ranked by what they cost you today.

    • Every message is accounted for

      Response times, volumes by type, what came in late and where it stalled.

    • It ranks what to automate

      Task type, volume, handling time, loaded cost. Ordered by what automating it is worth.

    • The flow, not the person

      Aggregated by process and by team. Individual metrics only if you deliberately turn them on.

  6. 06 Hand off

    Only automate what matters

    Approved orders land in your ERP.

    Any inbound format becomes a validated record, booked into your ERP through a certified write path. You set the approval gates, and they loosen as the touchless rate is proven.

    • Your numbers choose the processes

      We measure the real volumes first, read‑only. Automation starts where they say it pays.

    • Any format in

      PDF, email body, spreadsheet. All of it becomes one validated record.

    • You set the gates

      Approve every order, or only the exceptions. Your call, per flow.
