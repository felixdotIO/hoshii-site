Empowering humans to do more.

We are on a mission to put AI in people’s hands, so they can stop drowning in manual, repetitive work and spend their day on what actually matters: customers, judgement and growth.

Hoshii was founded on one conviction: the inbox was never built to run a business, and the people running theirs out of one deserve a real workspace.

Built close to the work.

Hoshii is headquartered in Zürich. Before this we built software at some of the world’s largest technology and pharmaceutical companies; now we build for the businesses that keep goods moving. What we share is a respect for the unglamorous work, and the conviction that it deserves better software.
